02 — The basics

How does an eSIM work?

Exactly like a traditional SIM card — just digital. Once you activate your data plan, your eSIM connects to the fastest local network at your destination with speeds up to 5G. Same WhatsApp number, same apps — only fast, reliable internet.

Nothing really changes. Keep your number, keep your apps, keep your chats. Your eSIM simply adds a travel data line to the phone you already carry — and hangs up when you get home. Just remember to check that your device is eSIM-compatible.

03 — Setup, simplified

How to install your eSIM

Three ways to get connected. Pick the one that matches your device — most people use Automatic and never leave the app.

1 STEP 01

Open the Holafly app

After your purchase, download the Holafly app and go to My eSIMs.

2 STEP 02

Tap “Install eSIM”

Then follow the on-screen instructions* — the phone does the rest.

3 STEP 03

Land & go online

That’s it! Your data will activate automatically at your destination.

* *Automatic installation is available on iOS 17.4+ and Android 10+. Don’t uninstall your eSIM — you can use it again on future trips.

1 STEP 01

Find your QR code

It’s waiting in the Holafly app or the instruction email we sent you.*

2 STEP 02

Press & hold, or scan

Press and hold the QR code to install (iOS 17.4+), or scan it with your mobile device.

3 STEP 03

Add eSIM

Tap Add eSIM, follow the instructions — your data activates at destination.

* *Need a device to scan with? A friend’s phone or a laptop scan of the emailed code both work.

1 STEP 01

Find your codes

Your manual activation codes live in the Holafly app or your instruction email.

2 STEP 02

Open SIM settings

Go to Settings > Connections > Mobile Network (or SIM Manager) and tap Add eSIM / Add Mobile Plan.

3 STEP 03

Enter details manually

Select Scan QR code, then tap Enter details manually and type in your codes. Data activates at destination.

! Menus vary slightly by brand: look for “SIM Manager” on Samsung or “Network & Internet” on Pixel.
